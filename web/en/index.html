Can I unlock them by leveling up?

Yes. Each pack opens for free once any hero reaches its level — Dungeon at Lv.10, Samurai at Lv.34, Ninja at Lv.40, and so on. Levels rise the more steadily you use your limits; even using the full weekly limit, Ninja takes about 4–5 months. Buying is a shortcut for people who want them right away.

How do I apply it?

In the app, buy under Customize → Buy premium packs, copy the key you receive, then choose Unlock with the copied license key. That's it.

Can I get a refund?

Within 14 days of purchase, we refund with no questions asked, and the key is deactivated automatically. For questions, open a GitHub issue.
